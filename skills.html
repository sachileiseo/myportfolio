Common problems

Content issues that quietly hurt rankings

Most of these show up in an audit before a single new page gets written.

✕Thin or duplicate content across pages
✕Cannibalized pages targeting the same keyword
✕Missing or repeated meta titles
✕Weak internal linking between related pages
✕Heading structure that skips levels or repeats H1s
✕Inconsistent voice across a site's pages
✕Keyword stuffed into copy instead of written naturally
✕No content plan tied to search intent
✕Outdated stats or claims left unchecked
✕Content with no clear CTA
✕No structure for AI-generated answers to pull from
